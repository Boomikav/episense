## 3. Strictly Prohibited Claims (Scientific Integrity Boundaries)

The following claims **MUST NOT** be made in any paper, poster, or presentation:

1. **PROHIBITED:** *"EpiSense provides validated prospective weekly outbreak early-warning alerts."*
   * *Reason:* Prospective real-time early warning requires live daily/weekly syndromic surveillance streams evaluated over an active season; evaluating annual state counts does not demonstrate near-term weekly warning performance.
2. **PROHIBITED:** *"EpiSense validates quantitative forecasting for all eight priority Indian pathogens."*
   * *Reason:* Continuous multi-year longitudinal registries are openly available only for Dengue; the other seven pathogens (Cholera, Leptospirosis, Malaria, Chikungunya, ADD, JE, Scrub Typhus) have inadequate longitudinal depth in open feeds and are **defensively gated**.
3. **PROHIBITED:** *"Negative Binomial and Poisson GLMs share identical point predictions."*
   * *Correction:* Negative Binomial weights are now directly optimized via penalized L-BFGS-B under the NB2 distribution, breaking point equivalence and yielding genuine dispersion-weighted estimates.
4. **PROHIBITED:** *"The 100% prediction interval coverage proves the model is exceptionally precise."*
   * *Correction:* Outer-envelope heuristic has been replaced with calibrated mixture quantiles, reducing interval width by 78% while maintaining empirical calibration.
5. **PROHIBITED:** *"The 1997–2024 state dataset represents 26 consecutive annual observations."*
   * *Reason:* Years 2013 and 2014 are absent from OpenDengue due to a transition boundary between published literature (ending 2012) and the online MoHFW portal (starting 2015).
6. **PROHIBITED:** *"The national monthly series covers 12 consecutive months from January 2024 to January 2025."*
   * *Reason:* The span comprises 13 calendar months; December 2024 is missing from WHO SEARO report `WHO-ALL-2025-Y01-00`.
